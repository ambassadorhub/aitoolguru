**What It Does:**

Jasper AI is built specifically for marketing teams and content agencies. It offers brand voice training, team collaboration, and SEO integration that goes beyond basic AI writing tools.

**Key Features:**

- Brand voice training for consistent tone
- 50+ marketing templates (ads, emails, blogs, social)
- SEO mode with Surfer SEO integration
- Team collaboration and approval workflows
- Campaign management and scheduling

**Pricing:**

- Creator: £39/month for individual use
- Pro: £59/month for teams with brand voices
- Business: Custom pricing for enterprise

**Verdict:** Overkill for solo operators, but excellent for UK marketing agencies and teams producing high content volumes. The brand voice consistency alone saves hours of editing. Not worth it if you're just writing occasional emails.
