**What It Does:**

Gamma is an AI presentation platform that replaces PowerPoint and Google Slides for business users who need professional decks without design expertise. Type a topic or paste an outline, and Gamma generates a complete presentation with layouts, images, and formatting in under a minute.

For UK small businesses, this solves the "death by PowerPoint" problem. Most SMEs send ugly, text-heavy slides to clients and investors, undermining their credibility. Gamma produces clean, modern decks that look like they came from a London design agency, at a fraction of the cost.

The platform also handles documents and webpages, so you can use the same tool for investor pitches, client proposals, training materials, and one-page websites. Everything is shareable via link, exportable to PDF, and editable in real time by your team.

**Key Features:**

- **AI deck generation:** Describe your topic, such as "quarterly marketing review for a Leeds-based recruitment agency," and Gamma builds a full presentation with titles, bullet points, visuals, and transitions.

- **Smart layouts:** The AI selects appropriate layouts based on content type. Data-heavy slides get charts and tables. Story-driven slides get large images and minimal text. No more blank-slide panic.

- **Real-time collaboration:** Multiple team members edit the same deck simultaneously, leave comments, and suggest changes. Eliminates version confusion and email attachment chains.

- **Interactive elements:** Embed polls, quizzes, forms, and call-to-action buttons directly into slides. Useful for training sessions, webinars, and sales presentations where audience engagement matters.

- **One-click themes:** Apply professional colour schemes and typography across the entire deck. Switch themes without manually reformatting every slide.

- **Export and sharing:** Share via link with analytics tracking, export to PDF for email attachments, or present live from the browser. No software installation required for viewers.

- **Document and webpage modes:** Beyond slides, Gamma creates long-form documents and single-page websites from the same editor. Use it for proposals, reports, landing pages, and course materials.

**Pricing:**

- **Free:** Unlimited personal decks, basic AI generation, Gamma branding on exports, and limited collaboration. Suitable for occasional use and testing the AI quality.

- **Plus: £8/month.** Removes Gamma branding, adds custom fonts, enables PDF export, and increases AI generation limits. The tier most individual business owners need.

- **Pro: £15/month.** Team workspaces, advanced analytics, priority AI access, and unlimited collaborators. Best for agencies, consultancies, and teams that produce decks regularly.

At £8 to £15 per month, Gamma costs less than a single hour of freelance presentation design, which typically runs £50 to £150 per project in the UK market.

**Verdict:**

Gamma fills a genuine gap in the UK small business software stack. Most companies waste hours wrestling with PowerPoint or pay designers for every pitch deck. Gamma delivers professional results in minutes, with AI that understands business context rather than just inserting generic templates.

Who should buy it: startups preparing investor pitches, agencies writing client proposals, consultants creating training decks, coaches building course materials, and sales teams that need polished presentations weekly. If your business lives or dies by the quality of your decks, Gamma is a no-brainer.

Who should skip it: businesses with established design teams using Figma, Adobe, or custom templates. If you already have a visual identity locked in professional software, Gamma's AI flexibility may conflict with your brand standards. Also, users who need complex data visualisation or advanced animation may find PowerPoint or Keynote more capable.

The real value is speed and consistency. Gamma lets a non-designer produce investor-grade presentations in the time it takes to drink a coffee. For UK businesses that pitch regularly, that speed advantage is worth far more than the monthly fee.
