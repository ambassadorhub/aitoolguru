**What It Does:**

Semrush is the most comprehensive SEO tool for UK businesses. Keyword research, competitor analysis, site audits, backlink tracking, and content optimization in one platform.

**Key Features:**

- Keyword research with UK-specific data
- Competitor analysis and gap identification
- Site audit for technical SEO issues
- Backlink analysis and building tools
- Content marketing platform with topic research
- Position tracking for local and national keywords

**Pricing:**

- Pro: £99/month for freelancers and small teams
- Guru: £191/month for agencies and mid-size businesses
- Business: £374/month for large enterprises

**Verdict:** The gold standard for UK SEO. Expensive but pays for itself if SEO is part of your strategy. Start with Pro; upgrade to Guru only when managing multiple client sites. The UK keyword data is more accurate than Ahrefs for local search.
