**What It Does:**

Copy.ai specializes in conversion-focused writing: sales emails, landing pages, ad copy, and product descriptions. It includes a workflow builder for automating entire marketing sequences.

**Key Features:**

- 90+ copywriting templates
- Chat by Copy.ai for conversational writing help
- Workflow automation for marketing sequences
- Brand voice customization
- Multilingual support for 25+ languages

**Pricing:**

- Free: 2,000 words/month
- Pro: £30/month for unlimited words and workflows
- Team: £50/user/month for collaboration features

**Verdict:** Best value for UK small businesses focused on sales and marketing. The free tier is genuinely useful, and the workflow builder automates entire campaigns. A strong alternative to Jasper at half the price.
