**What It Does:**

Claude, built by Anthropic, excels at long document analysis, nuanced reasoning, and maintaining context across extended conversations. Its 200K token context window handles entire reports, books, or codebases in one go.

**Key Features:**

- 200K context window for massive documents
- Superior reasoning on complex problems
- Artifacts feature for creating and editing content side-by-side
- More cautious and accurate on factual queries
- Excellent for legal, financial, and research analysis

**Pricing:**

- Free: Limited conversations with Claude 3.5 Sonnet
- Pro: £18/month for higher limits and Claude 3 Opus access
- Team: £20/user/month with admin controls

**Verdict:** Best for UK professionals who need deep document analysis or work with large datasets. The reasoning quality is noticeably better than ChatGPT for complex tasks. Worth the slight premium for serious business use.
