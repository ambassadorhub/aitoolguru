## The Bottom Line

UK recruiters face a perfect storm: skills shortages, high candidate expectations, and time pressure. AI tools that actually speed up hiring (not add complexity) are worth their weight in gold.

| Category | Best Tool | Starting Price |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| All-in-one ATS | **Workable** | ~£129/job/month |
| AI sourcing | **HireEZ** | ~£100/user/month |
| Video interviewing | **HireVue** | Custom pricing |
| Small business ATS | **Occupop** | ~£75/month |
| Enterprise ATS | **Greenhouse** | Custom pricing |
| CV parsing | **Textkernel** | API pricing |
| Candidate engagement | **Paradox (Olivia)** | Custom pricing |

## 1. Workable

**Best for:** Mid-size agencies, multi-job campaigns, collaborative hiring
**Rating:** 4.6/5

Workable combines a solid ATS with AI-powered candidate sourcing and screening. The AI features are practical, not gimmicky.

**What we like:**
- AI candidate sourcing from 200+ job boards
- Automated screening questions
- Interview scheduling automation
- Collaborative scorecards

**What we don't:**
- Per-job pricing adds up
- AI sourcing quality varies by role
- Limited customization

**Pricing:** ~£129 per active job/month.

**Verdict:** Best for agencies running multiple concurrent searches. The AI sourcing alone saves hours of manual searching.

## 2. HireEZ

**Best for:** Talent sourcing, headhunting, finding passive candidates
**Rating:** 4.5/5

HireEZ (formerly Hiretual) is an AI sourcing engine that finds candidates across the open web. It's like having a research team working 24/7.

**What we like:**
- AI searches across LinkedIn, GitHub, Twitter, and more
- Predicts candidate availability
- Automated outreach sequencing
- Diversity sourcing tools

**What we don't:**
- Requires LinkedIn integration
- Learning curve for Boolean builders
- Can feel impersonal if overused

**Pricing:** ~£100/user/month.

**Verdict:** Essential for headhunters and agencies sourcing hard-to-find talent. The time saved on manual searching is massive.

## 3. HireVue

**Best for:** High-volume hiring, structured interviews, assessment
**Rating:** 4.3/5

HireVue's AI video interviewing and assessment tools standardize evaluation and reduce unconscious bias.

**What we like:**
- Structured video interviews
- AI assessment of soft skills
- Game-based assessments
- Reduces interview scheduling burden

**What we don't:**
- Candidates can find video interviews impersonal
- AI assessment accuracy debated
- Enterprise pricing only

**Pricing:** Custom enterprise pricing.

**Verdict:** Best for graduate recruitment or high-volume roles. Not necessary for senior hires where personal connection matters.

## Building Your Recruitment Tech Stack

**Essential (under £200/month):**
1. Workable or Occupop for ATS
2. LinkedIn Recruiter Lite for sourcing
3. Calendly or similar for scheduling

**Advanced (£200-500/month):**
4. HireEZ for AI sourcing
5. Textkernel for CV parsing
6. HireVue for video screening

The sweet spot for most UK agencies is Workable + HireEZ. Everything else is nice-to-have.
