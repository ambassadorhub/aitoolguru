## The Bottom Line

AI writing tools have matured. The best ones now understand UK English, brand voice, and specific content formats. Here's what actually works.

| Category | Best Tool | Starting Price |
|----------|-----------|----------------|
| All-round writing | **ChatGPT Plus** | ~£16/month |
| Marketing copy | **Jasper AI** | ~£39/month |
| Grammar & polish | **Grammarly Premium** | ~£10/month |
| Long-form content | **Copy.ai** | ~£30/month |
| Academic/technical | **Claude Pro** | ~£18/month |
| Email sequences | ** Lavender** | ~£25/month |

## 1. ChatGPT Plus for Writing

**Best for:** Drafts, brainstorming, editing, creative writing
**Rating:** 4.7/5

ChatGPT is the Swiss Army knife of writing. It handles everything from email drafts to blog posts to creative fiction.

**What we like:**
- Understands UK English spelling and idioms
- Adapts tone on request (formal, casual, persuasive)
- Excellent for overcoming writer's block
- Fast iteration and rewriting

**What we don't:**
- Can be generic without good prompts
- No built-in plagiarism checking
- Requires fact-checking

**Pricing:** ~£16/month for Plus.

**Verdict:** The default choice. If you only buy one writing tool, make it ChatGPT Plus.

## 2. Grammarly Premium

**Best for:** Proofreading, tone adjustment, clarity improvements
**Rating:** 4.5/5

Grammarly's AI goes beyond spell-checking to suggest tone improvements, clarity rewrites, and engagement enhancements.

**What we like:**
- Real-time suggestions in any app
- Tone detection and adjustment
- Plagiarism checker included
- Works with UK English

**What we don't:**
- Can be overzealous with suggestions
- Premium features require subscription
- Sometimes misses context

**Pricing:** Free tier available. Premium at ~£10/month.

**Verdict:** Essential for anyone who writes professionally. The clarity suggestions alone improve readability significantly.

## 3. Jasper AI

**Best for:** Marketing teams, brand consistency, content at scale
**Rating:** 4.4/5

Jasper excels at producing large volumes of on-brand content. The brand voice training ensures consistency across all outputs.

**What we like:**
- Brand voice training
- 50+ marketing templates
- SEO mode with Surfer integration
- Team collaboration

**What we don't:**
- Expensive for individual users
- Learning curve
- Output needs editing

**Pricing:** Creator at ~£39/month. Pro at ~£59/month.

**Verdict:** Worth it for agencies and marketing teams. Solo writers should try Copy.ai first.
