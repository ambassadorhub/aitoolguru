Overview

Grammarly Premium is the gold standard for error-free, polished writing. In our testing over two weeks of real business use, we found it to be a standout performer in the writing category.

This review is based on hands-on testing with real business scenarios, analysis of 2156+ user reviews across Trustpilot, G2, and Reddit, and pricing verification direct from the vendor. We are not sponsored by Grammarly Premium. Some links may be affiliate links.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Catches errors that even careful proofreaders miss
  • Tone detector helps avoid miscommunication
  • Plagiarism checker included in business plan
  • Works across email, documents, and social media
  • Browser extension covers almost every platform
  • Clear explanations help you learn from mistakes

Cons

  • Aggressive suggestions can strip personality from writing
  • Premium price is steep for individual users
  • Plagiarism checker requires business plan
  • Occasionally flags correct British spellings
  • Some advanced features feel gimmicky
