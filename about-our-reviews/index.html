About Our Reviews

How we test, rate, and recommend AI tools. No vendor influence. No affiliate bias. Just honest evaluation.

Every tool on AI Tool Guru goes through the same rigorous evaluation process. We do not publish reviews based on press releases or vendor demos. Every tool is tested hands-on for a minimum of two weeks with real business scenarios.

We are independent. While some links are affiliate links, this never influences our ratings or recommendations. We regularly recommend tools with no affiliate programme when they are genuinely the best option.

Our Evaluation Criteria

  • Hands-on Testing

    Every tool is tested for a minimum of two weeks with real business scenarios, not just vendor demos.

  • Pricing Verification

    We check actual pricing pages, not headline rates. UK pricing is converted at prevailing exchange rates.

  • User Feedback Analysis

    We analyse reviews from G2, Trustpilot, Reddit, and customer support forums to understand real user experiences.

  • Competitor Comparison

    Every review includes direct comparison testing against the strongest competitors in the same category.

  • Long-term Value

    We assess whether a tool remains valuable after the novelty wears off. Will it still be worth it in six months?

  • UK Context

    We verify GBP pricing, test with UK English, and consider UK-specific regulations and data protection.

Our Rating Scale

Our ratings are on a scale of 1 to 5, with half-star increments. Here is what each range means:

  • Rating: 4.5 out of 5
    Outstanding

    Exceptional across all criteria. A must-have for most UK businesses.

  • Rating: 4 out of 5
    Very Good

    Strong performer with minor limitations. Recommended for the right use case.

  • Rating: 3.5 out of 5
    Good

    Solid tool that does the job. May lack polish or advanced features.

  • Rating: 3 out of 5
    Average

    Functional but not exceptional. Consider alternatives in the same price range.

  • Rating: 2.5 out of 5
    Below Average

    Significant limitations. Only recommended if no better option exists.

Pricing Transparency

Pricing is verified by visiting the vendor\'s pricing page and, where possible, going through the checkout flow to confirm the actual cost. For tools that bill in USD, we convert to GBP at prevailing exchange rates and note this clearly.

We always distinguish between:

  • Free plans: genuinely free tiers with real functionality
  • Free trials: time-limited access to paid features
  • Starting price: the cheapest paid plan that unlocks the features most businesses need
  • Business price: the plan most UK SMEs will actually need

Updates and Corrections

We review and update our content monthly. Every review page shows the last updated date. If you spot an error, a price change, or a feature update we have missed, please contact us and we will verify and update within 48 hours.

If a vendor disputes our review, we welcome their feedback. We will re-test the tool and either update our assessment or stand by our findings with a clear explanation.

Affiliate Disclosure

AI Tool Guru participates in affiliate programmes for some of the tools we review. This means we may earn a commission if you purchase through our links. This never influences which tools we recommend or how we rate them.

We regularly recommend tools with no affiliate programme when they are genuinely the best option. Our reputation depends on honesty, not commission rates. You will always see an affiliate disclosure on any page that contains affiliate links.
